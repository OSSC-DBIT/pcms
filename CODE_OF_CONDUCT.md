# Code of conduct

PCMS is built and maintained by the Open Source Student Club, Don Bosco Institute of
Technology (OSSC DBIT), for Don Bosco Institute of Technology, Bengaluru. This code
applies to everyone who works on PCMS: club members, DBIT staff and anyone invited to contribute.
It covers the repository, issues, reviews, chats and meetings about the project.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to software or to the project.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Explain the reason behind a review comment.
- Credit other people's work. Do not present someone else's code or ideas as your own.
- Keep commitments, or say early when you cannot.
- Follow DBIT's rules for student and staff conduct; this code adds to them, it does not replace them.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or threats, including about caste, religion, gender,
  language, disability, region or background.
- Sharing other people's personal information, including data from PCMS (names, phone numbers,
  photos, tickets), outside the work that needs it.
- Using access to PCMS, its servers or its accounts for anything other than maintaining it.
- Committing secrets, real staff or worker data, or deliberately weakening security.

## Data and access

PCMS holds data about DBIT staff and workers. Treat it as confidential. Use synthetic data in
development and tests. Production access is limited to the people named by DBIT and Team OSSC,
and every action there is audited.

## Reporting a problem

Report conduct concerns to the OSSC DBIT faculty coordinator or the club leads. If the concern
involves them, report to the DBIT Grievance Redressal Committee or the Internal Complaints
Committee. Reports are handled confidentially. Security vulnerabilities go privately to Team OSSC,
not to a public issue.

## Consequences

Depending on how serious it is: a private conversation, a warning, removal of repository or
server access, or referral to DBIT under its own procedures.
