# User guide

Open PCMS on the campus Wi-Fi at the address Team OSSC gives you, for example `https://pcms.dbit.edu.in`.
On a phone, tap **Install app** (Android) or **Share, then Add to Home Screen** (iPhone) to use it like an app.
Allow notifications when asked so you hear about updates. If you choose **Not now**, PCMS asks again after a
week. Turning notifications off in **Settings** stays off on that device until you turn them on again.

**First sign-in.** Use your college email and the temporary password from Team OSSC, then choose your
own password (at least 10 characters). If you forget it, ask Team OSSC to reset it; PCMS does not email
password links. Super admins also scan a QR code with Google or Microsoft Authenticator.

## Faculty

1. **Report**: choose what needs fixing and which part. If you choose **Other**, say what it is.
   Then choose the building, floor (Ground to Terrace) and room, write a short title, pick the severity
   (**Red** urgent, **Orange** soon, **Green** routine), and add up to 3 photos and 1 video.
   Cafeteria, Canteen and Parking are in the building list too. Block B has the Library; the Administrator
   Block has the Placement Department, Dean's Room, R&D Department, Board Room and Server Room. Fans,
   lights and other electrical faults are under **Mechanical / Electrical**.
   - **Camera** opens the camera inside PCMS. Choose **Photo** and tap **Take photo**, or choose **Video**
     and tap **Record**. A video stops by itself after 30 seconds. The round arrow switches between the
     front and back camera. If the browser cannot use the camera, PCMS opens your phone's camera app instead.
   - **Choose file** adds photos or videos you already have, including iPhone videos.
   - Location data is removed from every photo and video before it is stored.
   - You can write in English, Kannada or Hindi.
2. Tap **Submit report**. You get a token such as `C26-0001`. Keep it.
3. **My requests** shows the progress of each request, under **Open** and **Closed**. Search by title,
   token or room, and tap **Filters** to narrow by status, severity or type. Long lists are split into pages,
   and the counter tells you how many you have. You can add more photos or a video to an open request.
4. When the facilities team completes the work, inspect it and tap **Confirm completion**, or
   **Issue not resolved** and describe what remains.
5. After you confirm completion, the request is closed. Rate the service once with 1 to 5 stars and an
   optional comment. The rating is about the service, not an individual person.

Choose **English**, **ಕನ್ನಡ** or **हिन्दी** from the **Language** menu: in the sidebar, at the top of the
screen on a phone, or on the sign-in page. The choice is remembered on your device.

For a dangerous electrical fault, fire or flooding, call the campus emergency number first.

## Facility manager

1. **Queue** lists tickets in four tabs: **Needs action** (new reports, fixes to confirm, approved
   critical work that is ready to finish), **In progress**, **All open** and **Closed**.
   - The four counts at the top (New, Assigned, Update overdue, Awaiting approval) can be tapped to show
     just those tickets.
   - Search by title, token or room. Tap **Filters** to narrow by status, severity, type, building,
     department, or **Only late updates**. **More filters** adds who reported it (name or email), the
     reporter's role, worker or vendor, and a From and To date. Each filter in use shows as a chip; tap its cross to remove
     it, or **Clear** to remove them all.
   - Sort by **Severity** to see red reports first.
   - The filters stay in the page address, so refreshing or going back keeps the same list.
   - Every filter and all paging are done by the server, so the list is never quietly cut short.
2. Open a ticket and follow the highlighted step:
   - **Step 1, review this request.** Check the reporter's red, orange or green severity and tap **Accept**, or **Reject** and write the reason. The reporter sees the reason.
   - **Step 2, assign the work.** Contact the worker yourself by phone, WhatsApp or in person.
     PCMS sends no messages to workers. Record an optional worker or vendor name and tap **Mark as assigned**.
     For a postponed ticket the button is **Resume work**.
   - After assignment, the worker line has **Change** or **Add worker name** so you can update or
     clear the name later. Only staff see it.
   - If the worker starts later, tap **Mark work as started**. This is optional.
   - Record the **work requirements** if useful: one person, more people, parts or money up front.
     This helps the approvers decide. It is internal, so the reporter never sees it.
   - **Step 3, record the work.** Write what was done (or why it cannot be done yet) in the box. Under it
     are two buttons; press one **only if the work cannot be finished now**:
     - **Critical**: needs a spending decision. The box is the reason; add a cost estimate if known and tap
       **Send for approval**. It goes to the principal, or straight to institute management if the
       principal reported the problem.
     - **Postpone**: cannot be done now. The box is the reason; pick the date to review it again.
     - **Neither**: the work is done. The note is optional. Enter the **Actual cost (Rs)** if money was
       spent (exact amount, optional; the reporter never sees it), then tap **Mark as complete**.
       Press a button again to undo the choice.
   - The reporter is then asked to confirm completion. Once closed, they can rate the service.
3. **More actions** has change type, duplicate, cancel and reopen.
   - **Photos and video** are the reporter's evidence. You can open and play them, but not add to them.
   - Put your own files under **Documents**: PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text files or photos (for example
     a bill), up to 10 MB each and 10 per ticket. Files with macros or scripts are refused. Documents you
     add are seen only by you, the principal and institute management, never by the reporter or other
     facility managers.
   - **Staff messages** are shared by the facility manager, management, the principal and Team OSSC.
     The reporter never sees them.
4. **Your language.** Titles, descriptions, staff messages, reasons and history written in another language
   are shown translated into the language you chose, with **Show original** next to them. If the translation
   is not ready yet, tap **Translate**. Under the boxes you write in (report title and details, staff
   messages, reasons and notes) the **English / ಕನ್ನಡ / हिन्दी** buttons rewrite your text in that language
   before you send it; **Undo** brings your own words back. Translation is done by a machine, so check
   technical words, and open the original when something looks wrong.
5. **Notifications.** PCMS tells you about new reports, disputes, low ratings and reviews due by a
   notification on your phone or computer, never by email or SMS. Allow notifications when the app asks,
   or turn them on in **Settings, Notifications** on every device you use (tap **Send test** to check).
   On an iPhone, add PCMS to the Home Screen first.
6. **Reports** shows the period you choose with **From** and **To** dates: new, overdue, awaiting approval,
   repeat faults by place and type, tickets by building and department (with a bar for fixed and open),
   how long review and completion take, and service ratings. Filter by building, by department (with or
   without a building) and by type.
   **Download** gives the ticket list as CSV, an Excel workbook or a PDF report for the same period.
   Every download is recorded in the audit log.

Every staff member can also **Report an issue**, add photos and a video to their own report, and follow it under **My requests**.
A report by any staff member, including the principal, goes through the same steps as a faculty report.

## Principal

**Approvals** lists tickets the facility manager marked **Critical**. They come to you first. Read the reason (and
the estimate, if any), then choose **Approve** (with the approved limit), **Reject** (with the reason), or
**Escalate to institute management** (with the reason) when a higher authority must decide.
After approval the ticket returns to the facility manager, with **Step 4: Complete the approved work** open.
You can view every ticket and its photos, write staff messages, add documents, and see **Reports**, including
**Expenditure** (see below). You also see the documents the facility manager adds. You do not accept or assign work.

## Institute management

**Approvals** lists the requests the principal escalated, and requests on problems the principal reported
(those come to you directly), marked **With institute management**. Choose
**Approve**, **Request clarification**, **Defer** (with a review date) or **Reject**. Your decision is final.
You can also write staff messages, add documents, read the facility manager's documents, and see **Reports** and **Expenditure**.

**Expenditure** (principal, institute management and Team OSSC only) is on the **Reports** page. For the
From and To dates it shows the amount requested, approved and awaiting a decision in INR, the **actual cost
of repairs** the facility manager recorded, every request with its approved limit and actual cost, and a
breakdown by category. **Actual spending by department** shows a bar for each department, largest first,
with the amount and its share of the total. Switch the bars between **Actual cost**, **Approved** and
**Requested**. The table under it lists, per building and department, the requests raised, the amount
requested and approved, the repairs with a recorded cost and their actual cost. Choose a department at the top
of the page to see just that one. **Download** adds **Expenditure CSV** and
**Expenditure Excel workbook** (with a **By Department** sheet); the full Excel and PDF reports include
spending by department too. The facility manager does not see spending figures in reports.

Nobody, including Team OSSC, can approve a request they submitted or a ticket they reported.

## Super admin (Team OSSC)

- **Admin, then People**: **Import file** accepts `.xlsx` or `.csv` with columns `email, name, role,
  department, position, password`. Only email and name are required; headers such as `Email ID`, `Full Name`,
  `Dept` and `Designation` also work. Leave password blank and PCMS generates one. The position is shown with the
  person but never changes what they can do; only the role does.
  Preview first, then download the credentials workbook (every temporary password, with department and position),
  hand each password over in person, and delete the
  file afterwards. **Add person** creates one account (department and position optional); **Credentials file** downloads every temporary password
  that is still valid (it asks for your own password and is audited). **Reset** issues a new temporary password
  (and can reset a lost authenticator). Once a person chooses their own password, it cannot be downloaded.
  **Suspend** blocks access at once.
- **Types** and **Places**: add new types (each with an unused token letter) and buildings with their floors.
  **Edit** renames one or changes its parts or floors. **Retire** hides it from the report form without touching
  any existing ticket; **Restore** brings it back. Token letters are never reused, even from a retired type.
- **Audit log** lists every recorded change, newest first, filterable by action and entity.
  It is append-only in the database, so this screen can only read it.
- The super admin can also do everything the other roles can.
- **Remove ticket** (under **More actions** on any ticket, super admin only) takes it out of every list,
  report and export, for example a test entry or a duplicate that should not be counted. Give a reason.
  Nothing is erased: the history and the audit log keep who removed it and why.
